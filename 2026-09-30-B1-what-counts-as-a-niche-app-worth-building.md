# What Counts as a Niche App Worth Building

By **Joshua Chen** · [About / experience](2026-09-30-about-joshua-chen.md) · urbanu619@gmail.com


“Niche” does not mean tiny forever or weird for its own sake. A build-worthy niche usually has a sharp user, a repeating job, and a path to charge without a social network on day one.

Use this filter before you open an IDE — and before you fall in love with a marketplace zip. Hub: [buy or build](2026-09-30-A0-buy-or-build-decision-framework.md).

---

## Worth-building traits (stack them)

- Clear boundary on who it is for
- Purpose fits in one sentence
- Fixes a **repeating**, concrete hassle
- General tools leave this detail broken
- MVP shippable by 1–2 people
- Search / intent is strong when people look
- Data accumulation raises switching costs
- Natural paid hooks: reminders, history, collab, export, analysis
- Not hostage to one ban-prone third-party API
- No need for a large social graph on day one

Miss several of these and you are building a demo, not a product.

---

## Super-vertical beats vague categories

| Vague | Sharper |
| --- | --- |
| AI Fitness App | Hair Growth Tracker — fixed-angle photos, aligned timeline for transplant / treatment / grow-out |
| Personal Knowledge Base | Screenshot Recall — search and understand the screenshots you already hoard |

Vertical wins because landing page, store keywords, short demo video, pricing story, and community all get easier.

---

## Eight gate questions

Answer in writing:

1. Who hits this weekly (or daily)?
2. What clumsy workaround do they use now?
3. Do they already pay for adjacent solutions?
4. Can first use deliver value in ~60 seconds?
5. Is there a low-cost channel (search, community, niche media, industry)?
6. Does core value still hold **without** a large language model?
7. If someone clones the UI, do data, workflow, or distribution still protect you?
8. Are platform, copyright, privacy, and API risks controllable?

Two or more weak answers → park the idea or shrink the wedge.
