# How to Find Undervalued Demand (Before You Build or Buy)

By **Joshua Chen** · [About / experience]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link about.md %}) · urbanu619@gmail.com


Undervalued demand is not “a cool idea.” It is a repeated complaint with weak supply, or a failed product whose **job** still exists.

This is a research method. After you find a signal, route through [buy or build]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link buy-or-build.md %}) — many “new ideas” already have a dying listing attached.

---

## Why failure and complaint data beat success theater

- Winner case studies hide survivor bias
- Failures surface acquisition, pricing, platform, and cash-flow breakage
- The same death pattern across a niche becomes a rule you can screen with
- Timing, channels, or tech may have changed enough that a dead product’s job is alive again
- Domains, code, users, or content from failures can still be assets — or warnings

Do not invent a founder’s motive. Separate: what they said, what press said, what metrics show, what reviews show, what you infer.

---

## Build a narrow radar (not a firehose)

Pick one monitoring lane and go deep:

| Radar | Watch | You are looking for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NicheHOT | Reddit threads, 1-star clusters, PH launches, GitHub issues, search trends | Sharp jobs-to-be-done |
| AppHOT | Store newcomers, rank jumps, review spikes | Pull and breakage |
| DevHOT | Popular repos going stale | Maintenance gaps / acquire-or-fork clues |
| App Rescue Feed | Listings, price cuts, “shutting down” posts | Buy candidates |

Output is **opportunity cards with evidence**, not 100 random pitches a day.

---

## The opportunity card (minimum fields)

```text
Problem (one sentence)
Who complains
How often the complaint appears
Current workarounds
Competitors and their 1-star themes
MVP that could ship in weeks
Distribution channel that already exists
Platform / legal risk
Is something already for sale that maps to this job?
```

If the last line is “yes,” open the [marketplace guide]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link where-to-buy.md %}) before you open Figma.

---

## Five places undervalued demand hides

1. **Workaround threads** — spreadsheets, notebook photos, “I built a janky script…”
2. **1-star themes on big apps** — the niche slice the giant will not serve
3. **Shutdown posts** — “we’re sunsetting X” with users begging for export or a replacement
4. **Stale category leaders** — last update years ago, still ranking, reviews begging for basics
5. **Failed launches with clear jobs** — wrong channel or timing, not a fake problem

Cemetery-style datasets (killed SaaS, abandoned apps, failed crowdfunding, dead games) are useful when structured. The product is the **structured failure**, not a chatbot on top of a scrape.

## From signal to path

```text
Strong complaint cluster
  ├─ Live transferable Type A/B listing? → diligence (A1) → buy/rescue
  ├─ Only Type C / gray inventory? → build clean (or walk)
  └─ No inventory, clear MVP + channel? → build
```

Idea sparks (not validation): [idea pool]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link niche-idea-pool.md %}).

## Anti-patterns

- Generating ideas with no complaint URL attached
- Treating “AI for X” as a niche without a workflow
- Ignoring platform risk because the scrape “works today”
- Falling in love with a cemetery story and skipping [exclusions]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link what-not-to-buy.md %})
