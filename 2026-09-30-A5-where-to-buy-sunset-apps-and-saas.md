## Platform notes (buyer lens)

### Acquire.com

- **Fit:** Relatively mature online businesses and SaaS; stronger process than pure classifieds.
- **Buyer access (public pricing, 2026-09-30):** Browse public info free (Basic). Paid plans for private details / founder chat — Premium about **$390/year** (listings up to a stated revenue/size band), Platinum about **$780/year** (all sizes). Confirm current bands on [Acquire buyer help](https://help.acquire.com/buyer-faqs-1) / pricing.
- **Seller side (affects what you see listed):** Monthly listing fee roughly **$25–$100** by ask price band + closing fee roughly **6–8%** on successful sale ([seller pricing](https://acquire.com/seller-pricing/)).
- **Watch:** Unlocking details is not diligence. Still demand read-only Stripe/store proof.

### Flippa

- **Fit:** Broadest surface — websites, apps, SaaS, content, domains. Good for scanning; noisy for underwriting.
- **Buyer access (public pricing, 2026-09-30):** Free browsing; **Flippa Premium** marketed around **$49/month** or **~$490/year** for early access and extras ([Flippa pricing](https://flippa.com/pricing)). Closing rails (FlippaPay / Escrow.com) advertise fees from roughly **1% / 1.2%** — confirm at close.
- **Seller side:** Upfront listing packages (entry packages advertised from about **$29**) + **success fee** on sale (public copy cites success fees starting around **3–5%** depending on package/path; broker-led tiers quote higher percentages such as **6–10%**). Always open the live pricing matrix before modeling all-in cost.
- **Watch:** Seller-provided metrics vary wildly. Prefer verified-performance badges and still run your own checklist.

### Microns

- **Fit:** Micro-SaaS, small apps, newsletters, extensions — indie check sizes.
- **Buyer access (public pricing, 2026-09-30):** Free sees public details (deal flow may be delayed). **Premium ~$299 per 12 months** for private details, chat, transfer tooling ([Microns pricing](https://www.microns.io/pricing)).
- **Seller side:** No listing fee in public materials; success commission about **10% / 8% / 6%** by sale-price band ([sell page](https://www.microns.io/sell-your-startup) / [terms](https://www.microns.io/terms)).
- **Watch:** Small absolute dollars still need full transfer and churn checks.

### SideProjectors

- **Fit:** Side projects, experiments, source-heavy listings. Many are **not businesses**.
- **Fees (public help, 2026-09-30):** Site states listing is free and they **do not take commission** on sale; they also **do not run payment/escrow** — you arrange transfer and should use a third-party escrow ([help](https://help.sideprojectors.com/about)).
- **Watch:** Price as Type C by default until proven otherwise. "2–3× annual revenue" folklore on community pages is not underwriting.

### Transferslot

- **Fit:** Small profitable side projects; thinner inventory.
- **Fees:** Re-check the site on use day — do not rely on third-party roundups.
- **Watch:** Low liquidity means fewer comps; diligence burden stays on you.

## How to use marketplaces without getting farmed

1. Tag every listing **A / B / C** before you open a chat.
2. Never bid from the marketplace one-liner — use the [checklist](2026-09-30-A1-micro-app-acquisition-due-diligence-checklist.md).
3. Prefer venues that make **read-only verification** socially normal; leave venues that normalize screenshot-only deals for Type C shopping only.
4. Model **all-in cost**: purchase + buyer subscription + escrow + [migration hours](2026-09-30-A2-cost-of-taking-over-someone-elses-codebase.md).
5. Low asking multiple is not a gift — read [why multiples compress](2026-09-30-A6-why-micro-saas-sells-at-low-arr-multiples.md).

Official entry points also listed in research notes: Acquire, Flippa, Microns, SideProjectors, Transferslot.
