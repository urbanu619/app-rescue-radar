## Shared substrates (build once, brand many times)

These ideas collapse into a few engines:

- Photo / screenshot / email ingest
- OCR → structured fields
- Timelines
- Expiry / maintenance / cycle reminders
- Personal or household item libraries
- Auto-generated shareable or submit-ready packets
- Privacy-first local search
- Fixed-angle image alignment and change views

Ship **one sharp front door**, not a vague “life OS.”
