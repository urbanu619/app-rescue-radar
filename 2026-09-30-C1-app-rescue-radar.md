---

## What you get

### 1. Screening

Opportunity-style cards from public listings and shutdown signals:

```text
Listing / source
Ask context (no fantasy multiples)
Revenue / users — verified or not
Traffic quality hints
Stack and dependencies
Transfer feasibility (store, domain, subs, APIs)
Main risks
Fix hypotheses (30 / 60 / 90 days)
Evidence still required from the seller
```

Scoring dimensions (filters, not magic totals): distribution, revenue quality, users, transfer, platform dependency, legal/IP, tech health, fit to *your* fix skills, price sanity, seller credibility.

### 2. Rescue engineering

When the asset is Type A/B but the codebase or migration is the blocker — takeover, stabilize, rewrite the lethal parts. See the cost framing in [taking over a codebase](2026-09-30-A2-cost-of-taking-over-someone-elses-codebase.md).

### 3. Managed operations

After close: monitoring, dependency hygiene, support load, release cadence — the anti-“passive income” layer. Monthly engagement, scoped in writing.
